Straight answers

Before you call.

Are the media, events & tech names really all for sale?

Yes. Every name in this portfolio is for sale, including the ones with live sites on them. There is no reserve list and no hidden tier. Call and ask about any of them.

Why are there no prices on this page?

Because the right number depends on what you are doing with it. A name bought bare, a name bought with a site built on it, and a name bought as part of a cluster are three different conversations. Every price here is negotiable and quoted by phone.

What if the media, events & tech name I want is not in the list?

Then somebody else probably owns it, and that is a solvable problem. Our acquisition service researches ownership and negotiates on your behalf. If the name is still unregistered, you can register it new instead.

Can I buy more than one?

Often that is the smarter move. Owning a name and its close variant keeps a competitor from owning the near-miss, and buying a cluster across one market costs less per name than buying them one at a time. Ask about the whole media, events & tech group.
