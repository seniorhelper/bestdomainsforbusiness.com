Before you call.

Are all of these Movers names really for sale?

Yes. Every name in the portfolio is for sale, including the ones with live sites on them. There is no hidden reserve list.

Why are there no prices on the page?

Because the right number depends on what you are doing with it. A name bought bare, with a site built on it, or as part of a cluster are different conversations. Every price is negotiable and quoted by a person.

How fast can I have the domain?

Once payment clears, you get the authorization code and transfer instructions the same business day. The registrars control the transfer itself, which typically takes a few days for a .com. How transfers work.
