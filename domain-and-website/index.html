Straight answers

Website questions.

What does $99 a month actually get me?

A custom-coded website — not a template with your logo dropped in — built around the name, structured for search and for AI engines, with a conversion path that makes sense for your business. Rates go up from there with size, complexity and how much optimization work is involved. The honest answer on your number comes after we know what you are building.

Is it a lease or am I buying it?

It is structured as a lease with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months, priced off the site’s valuation at that time. If you would rather buy it outright from day one, that is a conversation we are happy to have instead.

Do I own the domain during the lease?

That depends on how the deal is structured and it is one of the first things we settle in writing, not something to leave vague. Ask on the call and you will get a direct answer about your specific arrangement.

Will it rank?

We will not promise that, and you should not work with anyone who does. Google’s own hiring guidance says nobody can guarantee a number one ranking, and algorithms move without warning. What we commit to is treating your business like our own, giving you the technology and the marketing skill we have, and working it honestly.

Can you work on WordPress, Shopify or Wix instead?

Yes. We specialize in custom-coded sites, but if you already run WordPress, Wix, Shopify or Webflow and you like it, we will work in it. We are not going to push a migration you did not ask for.

What if I already have a site?

Then a rebuild may not be the right call at all. Often the existing site can be improved instead, which is cheaper and faster. We will look at structure, speed, content, visibility and the conversion path before recommending anything expensive.

Tell us what you sell and where. You will get a straight recommendation on the name, the build, and whether you even need a new site at all.
