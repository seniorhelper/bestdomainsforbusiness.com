Privacy

Last updated September 2026. Short, because we do not collect much.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site: your name, phone number, email address, and whatever you write in the message. Nothing else. There is no account to create and no login.

What we do with it

We use it to answer you. That is the entire purpose. We do not sell it, rent it, trade it, or add you to a marketing list you did not ask for.

The lookup and valuation tools

The ownership lookup and the domain check on this site run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to us or stored anywhere. When you click through to a public lookup service, you are on their site under their privacy policy, not ours.

Forms

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form service, which processes the message in order to deliver it. Anti-spam measures on our forms are technical only and do not profile you.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not run third-party advertising trackers. If you dismiss the chat assistant, that preference is stored in your own browser so it stays dismissed — that value never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

Third-party links

Some links go to other sites, including hosting and lookup providers, and some of those are affiliate links. Once you leave this site you are covered by that site’s privacy policy.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer you and keep a normal business record of the conversation. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Colorado residents have rights under the Colorado Privacy Act and we honor those requests regardless of where you live. Call 1-800-481-8638 or write to info@eyetoad.com.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
