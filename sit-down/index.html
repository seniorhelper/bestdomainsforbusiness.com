The sit-down.

Five minutes, eleven slides, real numbers with sources, and one question at the end. This is the conversation we have with every business that calls about a name. Have a seat and we will walk through it.

yourbusinessname.comThe address is the search phrase.

Pull up a chair.

No pitch deck jargon, no pressure. Just what we have learned selling and building keyword sites since 2012, and the numbers behind it.

1 / 11

Let's start with the only number that matters.

What is one new customer worth to you, over a year? A roof is $12,000. A dental patient is $1,500 a year. A real estate closing is a five-figure commission. Hold that number. Everything from here is measured against it.

2 / 11

Where do those customers come from now?

Referrals, a truck wrap, maybe some ads. And search. When somebody needs what you do, they type it into Google or ask an AI assistant. The businesses that show up get the call. The ones that do not, do not exist to that customer.

3 / 11

Position is the whole game.

About 27% of clicks go to the first organic result, and the top three take most of the rest. Being on page two is roughly the same as being closed. That is not our opinion; it is the click-through data.

Source: Backlinko organic CTR study
4 / 11

Local intent turns into money fast.

Google's own research: 76% of people who search for something nearby on their phone visit a related business within a day, and 28% of those searches end in a purchase. The search is the visit.

Source: Think with Google, local search behavior
5 / 11

So what does a keyword domain actually do?

Honest answer first: it is not a ranking. Google has said an exact-match domain is not a shortcut, and it is far from the only factor. What it is: instant clarity. roofingcompaniesdenver.com tells a stranger what you do and where in under a second, and they remember it without trying. Clarity converts. Memorability repeats.

Source: Google Search Central: SEO starter guide
6 / 11

And it is the only ad you buy once.

Every other channel is rented. Stop paying, it stops working. A name you own keeps working in the search result, in the AI answer, on the truck, on the invoice and at a barbecue, whether or not you funded it this month. It sits on the books as an asset, not on the expense sheet.

7 / 11

Where it pays fastest.

Roofing, fencing, decks, painting. Dental, orthodontics, med spa, GLP-1. Auto glass, tires, mobile mechanics. Real estate. Beauty. Restaurants. Legal. Anywhere a single phone call is worth hundreds or thousands, a phrase-perfect name pays for itself with a handful of jobs.

8 / 11

The name is the foundation. The site is the building.

We are full-stack developers. We build custom-coded sites programmed to rank in Google and be cited by AI engines, with schema, speed, local signals and conversion copy done right, then we put the right name on the front of it. Same team, same phone number.

Source: Eye To Ad Media, Denver SEO
9 / 11

A quick question, if you will let me.

If your new sales funnel brought you an extra ten customers a week, what would that do to your business? Not to the website. To the business. The hires, the second truck, the month you finally take off. That is the number we are actually building toward.

10 / 11

About price.

Some domain brokers charge obscene figures for great names, then charge again to build anything on them. We do not publish prices because the right number depends on what you plan to do with the name, and we would rather negotiate something fair on the phone than post a number designed to anchor you. Sites start at $99 a month with a buyout option.

The short version, for the record

If you would rather read than sit.

Rankings decide who gets the call. About a quarter of clicks go to the first result; page two might as well be closed. Local searchers act within a day. If your business is not visible at the moment of the search, it is not considered.

A keyword domain is clarity, not a ranking. Google says exact-match names are not a shortcut, and they are one small factor among many. What the name does is tell a stranger what you do and where in a second, stick in memory, and repeat itself every time it is said. Clarity converts; memorability compounds.

It is the only ad you buy once. Ads, listings and leads are rented. The name is owned, and it appreciates or it does not, but it cannot be taken away.

It pays back fastest in high-ticket local trades. Roofing, fencing, decks, painting, bath and walk-in tubs, dental, orthodontics, med spa, GLP-1, auto glass, tires, mechanics, real estate, beauty, restaurants, legal, moving.
