Seller questions

Before you send it.

Do you buy domains outright?

Sometimes. If a name fits categories we already work in — home services, accessible bath, real estate, travel, marketing — we may make you a direct offer. That is the fastest possible outcome for you: one buyer, no listing, no waiting.

What if you do not want it?

Then we will tell you plainly, and we can broker it instead. Brokerage means we look for the buyer for whom your name solves a real problem, rather than listing it and hoping. Terms depend on the name and we agree them before any work starts.

What does a valuation cost?

Nothing. Send the name through the valuation page and you get a written read for free, including when the honest read is that it is worth less than you were hoping.

Should I use an auction site instead?

Auctions work well for names with broad appeal and a lot of interested parties. They work badly for a name whose value sits with one specific business, because that business has to happen to be watching on the right day. Most keyword and geo names fall into the second group.

How does the transfer work if it sells?

Payment clears first, then you release the authorization code and the name pushes to the buyer’s registrar. For larger transactions, escrow protects both sides and we will recommend it without being asked.

Rather just read it to us?

Call and say the name. We will tell you on the spot whether it is something we would buy, something we would broker, or something you should keep.
