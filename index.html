The part nobody else says out loud

A keyword domain will not rank you by itself.

Google removed the ranking benefit of exact-match keyword domains years ago and has said so repeatedly since. We sell these names for a living and we are still telling you that, because the alternative is a sale we have to defend in six months.

What a good name actually does: it says what you do before anyone clicks, it survives a phone call and a truck door, it creates branded searches nobody can outbid you for, and nobody else can ever register it. Those reasons hold up. The magic-ranking story does not.

Straight answers

Before you call.

Is every domain really for sale?

Every single one, including the names with live sites on them and the ones we use every day. There is no hidden reserve list. Some will be expensive. Nothing is off the table.

Why are there no prices anywhere on this site?

Because a price without context is a guess. The same name costs one thing bare, another with a site built on it, and another as part of a cluster. Every number here is negotiable and given on a call, by a person.

What if the name I want is not in your portfolio?

Then it is either unregistered — in which case just register it and save your money — or somebody owns it, and our acquisition service goes and negotiates for it.

Will a keyword domain make me rank?

No, and we are not going to pretend otherwise. Google has stated that keywords in a domain earn no ranking on their own. A good name does other things that are genuinely valuable. The full explanation is here, with sources.

Can you build the website too?

Yes. Custom-coded, from $99 a month with a buyout option. Details here. We can also work in WordPress, Shopify or Wix if that is what you already run.

How fast can a domain transfer?

You get the authorization code the same business day funds clear. After that the registrars control the clock — a .com typically completes within a few days.
