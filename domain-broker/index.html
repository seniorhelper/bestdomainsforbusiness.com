Straight answers

Before you pick up the phone.

Do I have to call? Can I just buy online?

You have to call, and that is deliberate rather than an obstacle. The right price depends on what you are building, and a checkout page cannot ask that question. A five-minute call gets you a real figure and, just as often, an honest read that the name you want is not the one you need.

Why will you not publish prices?

Because a published number would be wrong in most cases. The same name costs one thing bare, another with a site built on it, and another as part of a cluster. Publishing a single figure would mislead more buyers than it helped.

What does a domain like mine usually go for?

We will not throw out a range here, because a range without your situation attached is a guess dressed up as information. What we will say is how to judge it: compare it against what you already spend each month renting attention, and against what it costs you if a competitor owns the obvious name in your market instead.

Can you guarantee the name will help me rank?

No. Google has stated that keywords in a domain earn no ranking on their own, and that nobody can guarantee a position. We sell these names and we still say it out loud. Here is the long version with sources.

What if you do not own the name I want?

Then we check whether it is simply available to register, which is the cheapest outcome and happens more than people expect. If somebody else owns it, that is our acquisition service — $1,500 to engage, 15% on close, and no promises about the outcome.

Do you take a commission on names you already own?

No. When you buy from the portfolio you are buying owner-direct. There is no broker in the middle, no auction premium and no third party who can change their mind halfway through. Commission only applies when we are going out to acquire a name from somebody else.
